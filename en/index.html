About the data

  • Who counts

    The Institute of Mass Information is a Ukrainian media NGO that defends journalists' rights. IMI has monitored Russia's crimes against the media since the first day of the full-scale invasion.

  • What is counted

    Only Russia's crimes against journalists and media in Ukraine. Other freedom-of-speech violations are counted separately in IMI's Freedom of Speech Barometer and are not added to this figure.

  • How it is updated

    Monthly: data is published as of the 24th. The figure is cumulative and covers the whole period since 24 February 2022.

  • How to read the categories

    The sum of categories equals the headline figure (993).
